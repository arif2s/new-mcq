📚 Help & Platform Guide

🩺 Topic Verification & Knowledge Hub Circles (① ② ③)

• Circle ①: Set created (if ② and ③ are grey, click ① or "Review Topics" to confirm the medical topic list).

🖱️ Topic Chip Controls

• Normal Text: Verified clinical topic (matched via ZIM dictionary/synonyms).
• Grey Text: Unverified/doubtful term.
• Single-Click: Toggle green outline & underline (selected for note generation).
• Double-Click: Permanently add false-positive terms to your Ignore List.
• Click Outside Popup: Auto-saves your partial topic review to Knowledge Hub.
